## 1. Executive Problem Synthesis (Origin vs Reality)

In [docs/origin.md](file:///c:/Users/asamoah/the-mainframe/docs/origin.md), the foundational premise of The Mainframe was recorded:
> *"A simple task app did some things well, but it could not hold the whole picture I wanted to keep track of. Plans and ideas were scattered across separate lists and whatever device or app was nearby... I did not want a strict schedule or a single ranking to declare which part of my life mattered most... Seeing everything unfinished at once can be heavy; the system should help me choose and continue, not make the whole list feel like a verdict."*

### Field Validation (What Real Searchers Are Experiencing)
Synthesizing community sentiment across Reddit (`r/productivity`, `r/ADHD`, `r/ProductivityApps`, `r/selfhosted`, `r/ObsidianMD`), user burnout stems from four distinct traps:

1. **The "To-Do List Shame Spiral" (Guilt Mechanics):**
   - Standard task managers (Todoist, TickTick) rely on red notification badges, overdue rolling dates, and streak counters.
   - When life gets chaotic or burnout hits, returning to an app with 47 red overdue tasks triggers immediate shame and anxiety. Users abandon the tool completely because opening it feels like a personal indictment.
2. **The "Setup Procrastination Trap" (The Modular Fallacy):**
   - Users turn to modular workspaces (Notion, Obsidian, Anytype) hoping to "build their perfect Second Brain."
   - Result: They spend 20 hours tweaking relational databases, YAML frontmatter, and canvas layouts instead of doing actual work. The maintenance overhead exceeds the utility.
3. **The "Scattered Parallel Tracks" Dilemma:**
   - Ambitious builders don't just have generic "tasks." They have **Courses** they are studying, **Build Ideas** they sketched, **Ventures** they are operating, **Goals** they are measuring, and **Acquisitions** they are saving for.
   - Forcing all of these into a single flat checklist collapses nuance; splitting them across 5 different apps fragments attention.
4. **The "SaaS Hostage" Backlash:**
   - Cloud tools lock personal life plans behind subscription paywalls ($8–$20/mo), telemetry surveillance, and mandatory logins.
   - If internet drops, or if an account lapses, personal history is inaccessible.

| *“Overdue Tasks (14)”* | **“Needs Attention (3)”** | Avoids triggering dopamine deficit and avoidance loops. |
| *“You missed these deadlines”* | **“Continue where you left off”** | Zeigarnik-effect open loop without punitive judgment. |
| *“Paste Import”* | **“Bring in an existing list”** | Action-oriented, removes technical terminology. |
| *“Urgent”* | **“High Priority”** | Lowers cortisol/anxiety response. |
| *“Connect to Server IP”* | **“Sync with Nearby Device”** | Moves mental model from infrastructure to direct utility. |
