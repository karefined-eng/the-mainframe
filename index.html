The Mainframe offers a third way: five pre-modeled life domains backed by local SQLite, ready the moment you install it. Frequently asked questions about The Mainframe

What is The Mainframe?

The Mainframe is an open-source, local-first personal management desktop and mobile app built with Tauri v2 (Rust) and React. It organizes scattered projects, courses, ideas, purchases, and goals across 5 distinct domains with 100% offline SQLite storage.

How does The Mainframe protect user data and privacy?

The Mainframe requires no user account, no cloud servers, and runs zero telemetry. All data is kept 100% local on your device's SQLite database, syncing directly device-to-device over encrypted local Wi-Fi or wired USB.

Why is The Mainframe ADHD-friendly with zero-shame Neuro-UX?

The Mainframe replaces guilt-inducing overdue red badges and broken streak mechanics with neutral blunt re-entry. Working memory queues are strictly capped at 3 to 5 items to prevent decision paralysis.

How does device synchronization work without a cloud server?

Synchronization is direct peer-to-peer over local network Wi-Fi or wired USB with ADB reverse tunneling using a short-lived 6-digit PIN. Data never leaves your physical local network.

The parts most landing pages leave out

We believe in radical transparency. The Mainframe is an early public release. Desktop builds are currently unsigned while we establish our code-signing foundation. Data stays local to your device, and synchronization requires both devices to be present on the same local network or connected via USB cable.
