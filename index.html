Built for people whose work does not fit in one list

Traditional productivity systems collapse every ambition into a single overwhelming to-do queue where urgent errands eclipse long-term mastery. The Mainframe solves this through domain separation. By segmenting your brain dump into five dedicated, parallel tracks—Certifications, Acquisitions, Goals, Build Ideas, and Ventures—you eliminate the cognitive drag of sorting through hundreds of conflicting tasks. Each domain operates with its own cadence and metadata, so researching a hardware monitor never competes with finishing an AWS certification or launching a side business. Built specifically for engineers, creative generalists, and neurodivergent builders with ADHD, it eliminates the dopamine crash of overdue alerts and preserves mental energy for deep, uninterrupted work.

How The Mainframe compares to Notion, Todoist, and Obsidian

Most productivity tools force a painful trade-off: either surrender your personal data to subscription cloud silos that lock features behind monthly fees, or spend weeks writing custom markdown plugins and YAML scripts just to organize basic projects. The Mainframe offers a third way: an out-of-the-box personal operating system backed by local SQLite. Unlike cloud task managers, it functions entirely without an internet connection or login screen. Unlike complex wiki databases, it requires zero setup or formula maintenance. You get instant desktop and mobile performance, zero recurring subscription fees, and private peer-to-peer sync that transfers data directly over your local home network or wired USB cable without touching an intermediary cloud server.

Paste, structure, decide, continue

Importing your scattered thoughts takes less than three seconds. Simply paste unformatted text, copied clipboard snippets, or bulleted lists directly into the rapid ingestion drawer. The Mainframe algorithmically organizes and routes every item into its proper home: courses to your learning track, gear to acquisitions, product ideas to build ideas, and deliverables to ventures. Once ingested, the calm triage dashboard highlights only your three to five most critical priorities using cognitive working memory thresholds. You make immediate decisions without navigating dense menu hierarchies, clear your mental RAM, and get straight to executing your highest-leverage work.

Frequently asked questions about The Mainframe

What is The Mainframe?

The Mainframe is an open-source, local-first personal management desktop and mobile app built with Tauri v2 (Rust) and React. It organizes scattered projects, courses, ideas, purchases, and goals across 5 distinct domains with 100% offline SQLite storage.

How does The Mainframe protect user data and privacy?

The Mainframe requires no user account, no cloud servers, and runs zero telemetry. All data is kept 100% local on your device's SQLite database, syncing directly device-to-device over encrypted local Wi-Fi or wired USB.

Why is The Mainframe ADHD-friendly with zero-shame Neuro-UX?

The Mainframe replaces guilt-inducing overdue red badges and broken streak mechanics with neutral blunt re-entry. Working memory queues are strictly capped at 3 to 5 items to prevent decision paralysis.

How does device synchronization work without a cloud server?

Synchronization is direct peer-to-peer over local network Wi-Fi or wired USB with ADB reverse tunneling using a short-lived 6-digit PIN. Data never leaves your physical local network.

The parts most landing pages leave out

We believe in radical transparency. The Mainframe is an early public release. Desktop builds are currently unsigned while we establish our code-signing foundation. Data stays local to your device, and synchronization requires both devices to be present on the same local network or connected via USB cable.
